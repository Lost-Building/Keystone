# KeyStone Offline Disc License Policy

Purchasing a game grants one active personal-use game license. It does not transfer copyright or grant permission to distribute unauthorized copies.

Where supported, the owner may convert an active digital license into an offline physical-disc license for personal use. The conversion package contains an offline, cryptographically signed entitlement certificate and does not contain the marketplace ownership key.

Once the owner activates disc mode, the disc becomes the authorized copy associated with that license. KeyStone disables digital download and digital launch rights for that license. Disc conversion does not create a second license.

The owner must not reproduce, distribute, share, bypass, extract, forge, or alter the disc entitlement or its technical protections except where applicable law expressly permits an activity that cannot legally be restricted by agreement.

Offline operation does not guarantee permanent compatibility or permanent access. Disc deterioration, loss, hardware failure, operating-system changes, and loss of compatible drives may prevent future use. KeyStone does not deliberately erase, corrupt, or damage user media when verification fails. Invalid or altered packages may refuse to launch.

Before activation, KeyStone must clearly disclose that digital access will be disabled and obtain the owner’s express confirmation. Any replacement, recovery, resale, refund, and ownership-transfer rules must be shown before conversion.

This draft requires review by qualified counsel for every jurisdiction in which KeyStone operates.
