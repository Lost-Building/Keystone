Digital game marketplace

KeyStone

KeyStone is a marketplace for one-time purchases of downloadable video game licenses. Players can buy eligible games, manage their library, and download games they own.

What we offer

Game purchases

Customers can purchase digital video game licenses through secure checkout.

Developer listings

Independent developers can list eligible downloadable games for sale on KeyStone.

Library access

Customers can access owned games through their KeyStone account after payment is confirmed.

Policies

Payments

Payments are processed through Stripe. KeyStone does not store raw card numbers or CVV codes.

Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed case by case. Digital purchases may be refundable when access has not been used or when required by applicable law.

Privacy

We collect account, purchase, and support information needed to operate the marketplace. We do not sell personal information.

Terms

Users may only upload, sell, or download games when they have the legal rights to do so. Developers are responsible for ensuring submitted content is lawful and authorized.
